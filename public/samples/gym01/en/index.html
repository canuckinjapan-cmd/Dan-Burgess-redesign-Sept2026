SAFETY

Built for Safety

Even without staff on-site, every hour is covered.

01

Access Control

Only registered members can enter. Smart access replaces keys and check-in desks entirely — no key-sharing, no tailgating.

02

24/7 Monitoring & Emergency Response

Security cameras cover the facility around the clock, monitored remotely. An in-gym emergency button connects directly to our monitoring center. AEDs are on-site.

03

Cleaning & Maintenance

Staff visit on a regular schedule to inspect equipment and clean the space. Anything off? Report it through the app anytime.

04

Late-Night, Solo-Friendly

Entry requires a registered phone, so there's no walk-in access for non-members. With round-the-clock recording and monitoring, training alone at midnight is no different from training at noon.
