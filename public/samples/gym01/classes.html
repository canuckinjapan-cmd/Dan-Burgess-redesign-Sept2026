Flexibility & Core柔軟性＆体幹

Yoga & Pilatesヨガ & ピラティス

Our Yoga & Pilates sessions combine ancient physical practices with modern core stabilization techniques. Focused on deep breathing, mindfulness, and precise alignment, this class is designed to decompress your spine, lengthen tight muscles, and build deep structural core strength that supports your posture all day long.

Perfect for all experience levels, each class is adapted to your individual flow and goals. Whether you are looking to regain flexibility, recover from high-impact sports, or find a sanctuary of mental clarity amid a busy schedule, our premium studio environment offers the perfect space to reconnect with your body.

ヨガ＆ピラティスセッションは、伝統的な身体の動きと現代の体幹安定化技術を融合させたプログラムです。深い呼吸、マインドフルネス、そして正確なアライメントに焦点を当て、背骨を伸ばし、硬くなった筋肉をほぐし、日常生活の姿勢を支える深層筋肉（インナーマッスル）を効果的に鍛えます。

High Intensity & Burn高強度インターバル＆脂肪燃焼

HIIT ProgramHIITプログラム

Our High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) program is a fast-paced, heart-pumping class designed to maximize calorie burn and supercharge your metabolic rate. By alternating between bursts of explosive exercise and short periods of active recovery, you will challenge your athletic limits and trigger the "afterburn effect," burning calories long after you leave the gym.

Led by our Head Trainer, this class uses varied movements including athletic drills, kettlebells, and bodyweight training to build cardiovascular performance while preserving lean muscle. It is efficient, demanding, and incredibly rewarding for anyone looking to push their endurance to the next level.

HIIT（高強度インターバル）プログラムは、短時間で限界に挑むことで消費カロリーを最大化し、代謝を劇的に向上させるエキサイティングなクラスです。爆発的な有酸素・無酸素運動と短時間のインターバル（休息）を交互に繰り返すことで、アスリートレベルの限界を引き出し、運動終了後も長時間にわたって脂肪燃焼効果が持続する「アフターバーン効果」を引き出します。

ヘッドトレーナー主導のもと、ケトルベル、自重、そしてファンクショナルトレーニングを自在に組み合わせて、筋力を維持しながら心肺機能を極限まで高めます。効率よく最大の成果を手に入れたいすべての皆様、ぜひこの興奮と達成感を体験してください。

Group Classesグループフィットネスクラス

Experience the infectious energy of a collective weight-training and aerobic program! Our Group Classes are designed around community, music, and shared motivation. Combining high-energy cardiovascular tracks with resistance exercises using dumbbells, barbell plates, and resistance bands, we turn hard work into an engaging, collaborative experience.

Training in a collective environment not only keeps you accountable but also guarantees a supportive, dynamic atmosphere. Under the careful guidance of our coaches, you'll master proper form while fueling your growth through group synergy. Together, we rise, sweat, and achieve our fitness goals.

仲間と同じスタジオ空間で競い支え合うことで、継続が力となり、限界を難なく超えることができます。専属コーチの正確なフォーム指導のもと、怪我を防ぎながらグループレッスンならではのダイナミックな高揚感と確実なボディメイク効果をご体験ください。

Personal Trainingパーソナルトレーニング

Get a certified expert by your side to fast-track your success. Personal Training is the gold standard of results-driven fitness at Takasaki Fitness. From elite athletes to beginners restarting their fitness journey, this customized track is the most efficient highway to body sculpting and strength development.

Physiotherapy & Conditioning整体フィジオ & コンディショニング

Bridge the gap between rehabilitation and peak physical performance. Our Physiotherapy & Conditioning clinic specializes in joint rehabilitation, postural alignment corrections, and deep tissue activation. By identifying muscle imbalances and skeletal misalignments, we deliver custom physical adjustments and movement therapies to dissolve pain and restore active mobility.

Whether you are dealing with chronic lower back tightness, desk-work posture fatigue, or recovering from a sports injury, our scientific approach goes straight to the root cause of the discomfort. Reclaim a pain-free life, restore full freedom of movement, and build joint resilience with our evidence-based, medically supported body restoration sessions.

慢性的な腰痛や肩こり、デスクワークによるストレートネック、スポーツでの痛みを解決。科学的な証拠に基づき、安全で無痛の関節可動域解放・コンディショニングプログラムを提供し、柔軟な関節と本来のアクティブな生活、怪我に負けない健やかな強さを取り戻します。
